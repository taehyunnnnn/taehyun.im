Hello world!  My name is Taehyun (Tei) Im. I'm a second-year computer science student at UTSC, specializing in software engineering, and a TA for CSCA08. Outside of school I'm a co-founder and CTO of SchooledUp Inc., an AI platform giving students back the hours school takes.

I was born in Seoul, South Korea, and moved to London, Ontario in grade 6. In high school I tutored friends in Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, and Physics through the peer tutoring club, played trumpet in the school band since grade 7, and worked as a lifeguard and swim instructor at the YMCA. I moved to Toronto in 2025 for university. I also draw and model in AutoCAD, SketchUp, and SolidWorks.
